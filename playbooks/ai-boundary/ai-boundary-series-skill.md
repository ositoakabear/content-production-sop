# AI 的边界 Series Skill

This skill is only for the `AI 的边界` WeChat series. Use `../../docs/editorial/wechat-general-writing-skill.md` for reusable writing, rich-text, and author-voice rules.

## 1. Series Identity

Public series name:

```text
AI 的边界
```

Do not use the earlier working name as the public series name:

```text
AI 越强，人还剩下什么
```

Preferred title pattern:

```text
AI 的边界｜01：AI 不是突然闯进生活的
```

Article covers should be quieter than the WeChat title. For article one, a minimal cover with `AI 的边界｜01` is enough.

## 2. Four-Article Structure

### Article 1

Role:

- Start from the author's 2017/2018 small-language-student experience with Google Translate.
- Show that AI did not suddenly enter life because of ChatGPT.
- Explain AI development as parallel routes, not a single linear path.
- End by handing the reader to the next question: `AI 不能什么？`

Do not answer the whole series in this article.

### Article 2

Role:

- Discuss `AI 不能什么`.
- Use Ma Zhaoyuan's book 《人工智能之不能》 only as light support, not as the main frame.
- Explain Turing machine, computability, Goedel incompleteness, and the halting problem in plain language.
- Clarify why `can be described` is not the same as `can be computed`.
- Avoid pretending the author fully controls the book's theoretical system.

### Article 3

Role:

- Discuss what may remain human when knowledge, execution, and routine output become cheaper.
- Use the author's 2025 DeepSeek-era conversation with a textile-industry business owner as a key example.
- Develop the distinction between intelligence and wisdom.
- Treat wisdom not as simple understanding, but as creativity, judgment, imagination, questioning ability, and personal agency.
- Include examples around prompting, cross-border ecommerce, discovering demand, creating demand, selective unlearning of experience, and the Tencent experience of successful organizations becoming too reference-driven.

### Article 4

Role:

- Do not use `AI has boundaries` as comfort.
- Turn the series into a practical ability framework.
- Discuss how individuals should train questioning, judgment, imagination, responsibility, and AI co-working ability.

## 3. AI Development Explanation

When explaining AI history in this series:

- Do not present behavior trees, machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, and generative AI as a clean replacement chain.
- Say they are multiple routes that developed and overlapped over time.
- Make clear that rules, search, behavior trees, machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, and generative models still coexist.
- Emphasize why ordinary people feel the shock only after generative AI became easy to use.

Reusable sentence:

```text
AI 的发展是多条路线的并行叠加，而不是简单的代际替换。
```

## 4. Tone Guardrail

The series should not deny AI, comfort the reader too early, or rely on `AI 没有人性` as an easy answer.

Recurring emotional center:

```text
AI 真的很强，所以我们才更需要认真理解它的边界。
```

The strongest framing:

```text
AI 的强大不是简单宣告人的失败，而是在逼迫我们重新分辨：哪些能力只是我们曾经误以为不可替代，哪些能力才值得长期训练。
```

## 5. Visual Preferences

Title cover:

- Minimalist.
- Keep only the series name and issue mark when possible.
- Place the text on the left side, vertically centered, not in the top-left corner.
- Keep `｜01` for article one.
- Avoid centered poster text that looks cheap.

Album cover:

- Minimalist 3:4 layout.
- Text: `AI的边界`.
- Move the main text slightly right if the visual center feels off.
- Use a simple AI-related icon or abstract chip/network symbol.

Development-route chart:

- Mobile-friendly vertical layout.
- Explain the routes clearly.
- Remove bottom creator-credit text if the user wants a clean final image.
- Do not make the chart look like a screenshot embedded in the article.

## 6. Known Article-One Lessons

Article one works best when it closes with a clean handoff to article two.

Good transition direction:

```text
但要回答“人还剩下什么”，不能只靠情绪，也不能只靠一句“AI 没有人性”。
我们得继续往前走，认真看看：AI 的能力边界到底在哪里。
```

Avoid ending both the previous section and the `NEXT` section with the same broad question.
