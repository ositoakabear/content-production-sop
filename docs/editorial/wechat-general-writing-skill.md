## 1. Approved Author-Voice Baseline

This is the primary voice baseline for Axiong's WeChat writing across original essays, Kickstarter product observations, and unicorn/company analysis. Vertical playbooks still control research requirements and article-specific structure; this section controls how the prose thinks, moves, and sounds.

The owner approved this baseline on 2026-08-08 from three high-originality samples:

- `梁文锋与 DeepSeek：一家中国 AI 公司的来路与选择`
- `多邻国不能让你学会一门语言？（一）`
- `多邻国不能让你学会一门语言？（二）`

Do not copy their surface wording or force every article into the same outline. Reproduce the stable reasoning habits behind them.

### 1.1 Voice Fingerprint

The shortest description of the voice is:

```text
个人触发 → 真实矛盾 → 分清两件事 → 机制与证据 → 承认边界 → 给出判断 → 留下下一步检验
```

The voice should feel:

- Personal but not diary-like
- Analytical but not academic
- Conversational but not casual to the point of looseness
- Willing to admire a product or company while still questioning its limits
- Clear about what is fact, what is inference, and what is the author's own judgment
- Slightly sharp, but never sharp merely to manufacture conflict

Write as a thoughtful participant with relevant lived experience, not as an omniscient media institution. A strong article may openly say, in effect, "I respect this product, but I still doubt this claim." That tension is often the beginning of the argument rather than a problem to edit away.

### 1.2 Default Reasoning Arc

Use the following as a reasoning sequence, not a rigid template:

1. Start with a concrete trigger: a report, a recent product experience, an earlier profession, a conversation, or a question the author genuinely cannot dismiss.
2. Explain why the subject feels contradictory or difficult to judge.
3. Turn that discomfort into one concrete question.
4. Separate two ideas that readers may have blended together.
5. Explain the mechanism with evidence and recognizable examples.
6. Admit the counterargument, valid use case, or limit of the evidence.
7. Compress the section into a plain judgment.
8. End with the next business, product, or human test instead of a vague prediction.

Not every article needs all eight moves, and their order may change. The important part is that the judgment is visibly earned rather than announced at the beginning and repeated until the end.

### 1.3 First Person And Epistemic Honesty

First person is evidence of perspective, not decoration.

Preferred:

- Mention the author's real background when it changes how the subject is understood: language study, game production, AI use, product work, regional experience, or prior research.
- Keep honest uncertainty when it increases trust: `这是我的主观直觉`, `目前公开资料只能说明`, `这一点我也不能确定`.
- Allow a small amount of self-aware humor when natural. One light aside can make the author visible; repeated jokes weaken the argument.
- Explicitly distinguish personal experience from general evidence.
- Preserve a useful rough edge in the author's phrasing instead of polishing every sentence into institutional prose.

Avoid:

- Inventing product use, conversations, professional experience, or emotional reactions to create an opening.
- Using `我认为` before every conclusion when the paragraph already makes the point of view clear.
- Turning limited experience into universal proof.
- Performing expertise the author does not claim to have.

### 1.4 Build With Tension, Then Draw A Boundary

The recurring argumentative move is not binary attack. It is boundary-setting.

Examples of the underlying logic:

```text
它是一家非常优秀的公司，但未必是一位优秀的语言老师。
```

```text
愿意打开，不等于真正学会。
```

```text
不是没有效果，而是被证明得比较充分的效果有明确边界。
```

For products and companies:

- Separate a good product from a complete solution.
- Separate user activity from user outcome.
- Separate a strong business from an unquestionable social or educational value.
- Separate an attractive story from a verified fact.
- Separate the company's commercial floor from its technical or strategic upside.

Give the strongest fair version of the positive case before stating the limitation. Criticism becomes more credible when readers can see what the author genuinely respects.

### 1.5 Concrete Distinctions Over Abstract Philosophy

When the argument becomes vague, identify the two things being confused.

Useful patterns include:

```text
不是 A，而是 B。
```

```text
A 很重要，但 A 不等于 B。
```

```text
更准确的判断不是……而是……
```

```text
这不意味着……它说明的是……
```

Use these patterns to clarify reasoning, not as a verbal tic. Do not stack several `不是……而是……` sentences merely to imitate the samples.

Concrete distinctions are preferred to phrases such as `终极价值`, `时代答案`, `重新定义`, or `更高维度`. If a claim cannot be translated into a product behavior, business mechanism, user scene, or observable test, it is probably still too abstract.

### 1.6 Examples Must Do Analytical Work

Use everyday scenes to make the difference between two concepts visible:

- Recognizing a sentence versus generating an answer in a real conversation
- Ordering a standard coffee versus adapting the request when the situation changes
- Completing a five-minute task versus building a durable skill
- A game check-in reward versus slow feedback from learning or fitness
- A free consumer entry point versus the part of a business that actually earns revenue

Analogies should preferably come from domains the author genuinely knows. After the example, state what it proves. Do not leave a story in the article only because it is vivid.

### 1.7 Sentence Rhythm And Section Architecture

- Keep most paragraphs to one to three sentences on mobile.
- Alternate explanatory paragraphs with short judgment sentences.
- Let a short sentence land after a longer mechanism explanation.
- Prefer section titles that already carry a claim, such as `但愿意打开，不等于真正学会`, instead of generic labels such as `产品分析` or `商业模式分析`.
- Use parallel `一方面……另一方面……` structures when they reflect a real split in the author's thinking, not to make every section look symmetrical.
- A rhetorical question should open the next piece of reasoning. Do not repeat the same grand question in the opening, body, and ending.

Pull-quote labels such as `判断`, `核心判断`, `商业模式`, or `关键检验` may be used when the following sentence deserves to survive outside its paragraph. Do not create a labeled card for every section.

### 1.8 Evidence Should Reveal Its Own Limits

Use `content-growth-optimization-standard.md` for the full factual-reliability gate. The voice-specific requirement is to make the scope of evidence readable in the prose.

Preferred moves:

- State what a study measured and what it did not measure.
- Attribute financing, valuation, roadmap, or anonymous-source claims to the exact disclosure or report.
- Mark an inference as an inference instead of hiding it behind authoritative tone.
- Explain one technical or financial term in plain language only when the reader needs it.
- Add a data cutoff and source note for research-heavy company articles.

Evidence should resolve a question or sharpen a distinction. A pile of numbers is not analysis.

### 1.9 End With A Test, Not A Slogan

The ending should return to the article's central distinction and identify what still has to be proved.

Preferred:

- A concrete next test: whether AI improves speaking ability without damaging margins; whether a product converts novelty into continued use; whether a company turns adoption into revenue.
- A broader human reflection only when it grows directly from the subject and the author's experience.
- One final sentence with plain forward motion.

Avoid:

- `未来可期`, `让我们拭目以待`, or an equivalent generic forecast.
- Repeating every section conclusion in a final recap.
- Raising a new grand philosophical question that the article has not prepared.
- Forcing mystery into an argument that is already complete.

### 1.10 Application Across Article Types

For original essays and owner-edited drafts:

- Protect the personal trigger, ambivalence, and small concrete details first.
- Optimize clarity and structure without replacing the author's thinking process with a generic media voice.
- When suggesting a major rewrite, offer tone options instead of silently choosing a more promotional version.

For Kickstarter product observations:

- Begin from a real user pain point, product scene, or the author's genuine product judgment; never fabricate hands-on experience.
- Respect the ingenuity before challenging necessity, usability, price, manufacturability, or long-term value.
- Separate `看起来有意思`, `解决了问题`, and `值得购买`.
- End with who the product is for, who should hesitate, and what campaign or delivery signal still needs verification.

For unicorn and company analysis:

- Do not default to an encyclopedia-like company chronology.
- Use history only to answer a present question about the founder, strategic choice, business model, financing, or competitive position.
- Keep subjective attraction or intuition when it is explicitly labeled, then test it against public evidence.
- Separate confirmed facts, media reports, transaction-implied figures, and author inference.
- Finish with the concrete business or organizational question the company has not yet answered.

### 1.11 AI-Like Phrasing To Remove

Revise when the draft shows several of these symptoms:

- Opens with `随着……` or a macro trend before the reader knows why the author cares
- Uses perfect three-part symmetry in every section
- Repeats the same conclusion with different abstract nouns
- Overuses `真正`, `本质上`, `某种程度上`, `不仅……更……`, or `不是……而是……`
- Turns a lived observation into inflated language about an era, civilization, or ultimate value
- Sounds certain where the source material is uncertain
- Uses a fake first-person anecdote to simulate author presence
- Ends with a slogan rather than an observable test
- Polishes away the small qualifications, humor, or blunt phrases that make the author recognizable

### 1.12 Voice Audit Before Finalization

Before calling a draft final, check:

- Can a reader identify why this author, specifically, wanted to write the article?
- Is there one real tension rather than a manufactured controversy?
- Does the article separate at least one pair of easily confused ideas?
- Are concrete scenes doing analytical work?
- Are admiration and criticism both represented fairly where relevant?
- Can the reader tell fact, inference, and author judgment apart?
- Does each major conclusion add something new instead of paraphrasing an earlier conclusion?
- Does the ending name a next test or boundary?
- After removing the title and brand name, does the prose still sound like Axiong rather than a generic business-content account?

If the last answer is no, revise the reasoning movement and concrete detail first. Changing adjectives is not enough.

## 2. Series-First Planning

When an idea may become a series, plan the series before overloading article one.

Recommended planning fields:

- Series name
- Four to six article titles
- One-sentence role of each article
- Which personal examples belong to which article
- Which theory, book, or technical concept should be used lightly rather than becoming the main axis

Article one should usually work as the doorway:

- Introduce the personal trigger.
- Explain why the issue matters now.
- Give enough context for new readers.
- Leave one clearly defined question for article two.

Do not try to answer the whole series in the first article.

## 3. Series Title Layering

For multi-part essays, prefer a stable series title plus issue number plus article-specific title.

Pattern:

```text
<系列名>｜<编号>：<本篇标题>
```

Keep three title layers separate:

- `WeChat title`: carries the full click promise.
- `Cover image text`: can be much simpler, often only the series name and issue number.
- `Digest`: states the core discomfort or question in one plain sentence.

Avoid putting the whole article title into the cover image when the WeChat title already carries it.

## 4. Ending And NEXT Blocks

The final body section and the `NEXT` block should not do the same job.

Use the final body section to close the current article's argument.

Use the `NEXT` block to hand the reader to the next article's specific question.

If both sections ask "人还剩下什么" or another broad existential question, revise one of them into a concrete transition.

## 5. Rich Text Editing Additions

Do not repeat the existing HTML packaging rules. This section only records additions from recent editing experience.

When generating a copy-helper HTML page:

- Keep a clean copy root, such as `#copy-root`, that contains only article content.
- Do not put title, digest, internal notes, or copy buttons inside the copyable article area.
- Maintain source HTML and copy-helper HTML separately when useful.
- Do not place screenshot-only editing notes into the final article body.
- If the user still wants to insert text, GIFs, or images manually, leave an intentional blank paragraph before the relevant section.

Recommended blank editing line:

```html
<p style="margin:0 8px 24px;min-height:1.75em;"><br /></p>
```

## 6. Visuals As Thinking Tools

Use visuals when they clarify thinking, not just to decorate the article.

Good use cases:

- A concept that readers may wrongly understand as a single straight line
- A series identity cover
- A comparison framework
- A transition between article one and later articles

For thinking essays, prefer minimal visuals and fewer decorative elements. Keep precise labels in editable text or captions unless the image text has been visually checked.
