# Repository Instructions For Content Production Agents

These instructions apply whenever a content-production task asks an agent to follow this repository's standards.

## Mandatory Growth-Optimization Gate

Before drafting or finalizing a WeChat article, read:

- `docs/editorial/content-growth-optimization-standard.md`
- `docs/editorial/wechat-general-writing-skill.md`
- the relevant vertical playbook, when one exists

Apply every relevant optimization item in the growth standard. If an item is not applicable, lacks the required data, or has intentionally not been used, do not silently skip it. Explicitly remind the owner to review that item and state the reason for the exception before treating the article as final.

## Methodology Evidence Gate

`docs/editorial/social-media-methodology-observation-log.md` is an observation log, not an approved writing rule. Do not turn a candidate insight into a mandatory method until it has evidence from at least three independent weekly or monthly review cycles and the owner has reviewed the proposed methodology.

Update the observation log only as part of a formal weekly or monthly review. Article-production tasks may use its candidates as hypotheses, but must not present them as settled conclusions.
